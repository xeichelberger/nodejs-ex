# Free Resource Strategy — Ecommerce

You are an expert in lead generation through free resources for ecommerce brands. Your goal is to design valuable free resources that capture email addresses, build trust, and drive first purchases.

**Before starting:** Read `brand/ashmi-brand-reference.md` and `.claude/commands/ecommerce-context.md` for brand context.

*Adapted from SaaS free-tool-strategy for ecommerce lead magnets and interactive content.*

## Free Resource Ideas for Baby Clothing

### Tier 1: High-Impact (Build These First)

**1. Style Quiz: "Find the Perfect Piece"**
- 5-7 questions: baby's age, upcoming occasions, color preferences, style personality
- Output: Personalized product recommendations
- Captures: email, baby age, style preferences (goldmine for segmentation)
- Leads directly to product pages
- **Expected conversion:** 30-50% email capture from quiz completers

**2. Baby Occasion Dressing Guide (PDF)**
- Beautiful, branded PDF guide
- "What to Dress Baby in for Every Occasion"
- Covers: weddings, christenings, family photos, holidays, first birthday
- Each occasion features Ashmi products
- Gate behind email capture
- **Expected conversion:** 15-25% email capture

**3. Size Guide & Growth Tracker**
- Interactive size finder (input baby's age/measurements → recommended size)
- Printable growth tracker (milestones + sizes)
- Captures email and baby age (for lifecycle marketing)
- **Expected conversion:** 20-30% email capture

### Tier 2: Medium-Impact

**4. Baby's First Year Photo Planner**
- Monthly photo ideas and outfit suggestions
- Milestone checklist
- Ties each milestone to an Ashmi piece
- PDF or interactive format

**5. Gift Guide by Budget**
- Under $25, Under $50, Under $80
- Organized by occasion
- Shareable (gift buyers forward to others)
- Great for Pinterest and Google traffic

**6. Fabric Care Guide**
- How to wash and care for premium baby clothes
- Makes the case for quality (pieces that last)
- SEO value for "how to wash baby clothes" queries
- Positions Ashmi as expert

### Tier 3: Ambitious (Future)

**7. Baby Wardrobe Planner**
- Interactive tool: "Build a capsule wardrobe for [season]"
- Drag and drop pieces into a wardrobe
- Shows what you need vs what you have
- Generates a shopping list

**8. Name Embroidery Preview**
- Upload baby's name → see it on a piece
- Creates endowment effect (feels "mine" before buying)
- High engagement, shareable
