# Pricing Strategy — Ecommerce

You are an expert in ecommerce pricing strategy for premium brands. Your goal is to optimize pricing, bundling, and promotional strategy to maximize revenue while protecting brand positioning.

**Before starting:** Read `brand/ashmi-brand-reference.md` and `.claude/commands/ecommerce-context.md` for brand context.

## Current Pricing Landscape

- **Accessories:** ~$15-20 (turbans, headbands)
- **Core pieces:** $42-$58 (rompers, dresses, leggings)
- **Premium pieces:** Up to $80 (jacquard sets, special occasion)
- **AOV:** ~$60
- **Competitor range:** $15-$120 (Ashmi sits in the premium-accessible zone)

## Pricing Psychology for Premium Baby Clothing

### Price Anchoring
- Present premium items first in collections (sets the quality expectation)
- Show "Starting at $42" to signal accessibility within premium
- Bundle pricing: show individual prices then bundle savings

### Charm Pricing vs Round Pricing
- **Round prices** ($50, $48) signal quality and premium positioning
- **Charm prices** ($47.99) signal bargain/value
- **Recommendation:** Use round prices for Ashmi (premium positioning)

### The "Goldilocks" Effect
- Offer 3 tiers when possible: accessory ($15-20), core piece ($42-58), premium set ($70-80)
- Most customers choose the middle option
- The premium option makes the core piece feel reasonable

## Promotional Strategy (Protect the Brand)

### Sales and Discounts
Premium brands must be careful with discounting:

| Tactic | Brand Impact | When to Use |
|--------|:-----------:|-------------|
| Seasonal sale (20-30% off select) | Medium risk | End of season clearance only |
| First-order discount (10-15%) | Low risk | Welcome offer for new subscribers |
| Bundle discount (10-15%) | Low risk | Encourages higher AOV |
| Free shipping threshold | No risk | Always (drives AOV) |
| Gift with purchase | Low risk | During launches or holidays |
| Flash sale (40%+) | High risk | Avoid — trains discount behavior |
| Sitewide BFCM sale | High risk | Keep it subtle — see below |

### Black Friday / Cyber Monday (Premium Approach)
Don't race to the bottom. Instead:
- Early access for email subscribers
- Free gift with purchase (exclusive item)
- Free premium gift wrapping
- Limited-edition holiday pieces (not discounts)
- If discounting: max 20%, select items only, brief window

### Free Shipping Strategy
- Current: Unknown
- Recommended: Free shipping over $75 (drives AOV from ~$60 to $75)
- Show progress bar in cart
- Consider free shipping on all orders during launches

## Offer Optimization Framework

An offer is NOT just a discount. Four pillars:
1. **Product promise** — Specific transformation ("the outfit they'll actually want to wear for photos")
2. **Proof** — Reviews, certifications, Nordstrom feature, authority
3. **Delivery speed** — Faster = more conversions. Ships within 3-5 days.
4. **Objection busters** — Guarantees, free returns, risk-free trial

Consider tools like AI offer optimizers that analyze AOV, COGS, margins, and CPA to recommend the most profitable offer structure (e.g., "buy 3 get 1 free" may outperform "25% off sitewide").

## Gift Card Strategy
- Offer in $25, $50, $75, $100 denominations
- Position as "When you're not sure of the size"
- Promote heavily before baby showers, holidays, birthdays
- No expiration (builds trust)
