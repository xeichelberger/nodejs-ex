# Referral & Ambassador Program — Ecommerce

You are an expert in ecommerce referral programs and brand ambassador strategies. Your goal is to design programs that turn happy customers into advocates, driving new customer acquisition at low cost.

**Before starting:** Read `brand/ashmi-brand-reference.md` and `.claude/commands/ecommerce-context.md` for brand context.

## Two Programs

### 1. Customer Referral Program

**How it works:**
- Existing customer shares a unique referral link
- New customer gets a reward (discount or free shipping)
- Referring customer gets a reward after purchase completes

**Reward Structure Options:**

| Model | Give | Get | Best For |
|-------|------|-----|----------|
| Discount both | New: 15% off | Referrer: 15% off next order | Quick adoption |
| Store credit | New: $10 off | Referrer: $10 credit | Higher perceived value |
| Free shipping | New: free shipping | Referrer: free shipping | Low margin impact |
| Free gift | New: free accessory | Referrer: free accessory | Product discovery |

**Recommended for Ashmi:** Give $10, Get $10 (store credit). Feels generous without deep discounting.

**Referral program copy (brand voice):**
- "Share something beautiful" (program name)
- "Know someone who'd love Ashmi? Share your link and you'll both get $10."
- NOT: "Refer a friend and earn CASH! 💰"

### 2. Made With Love Tribe (Ambassador Program)

Based on existing program structure:

**Tiers:**

| Tier | Requirements | Perks |
|------|-------------|-------|
| **Member** | Apply + approved | 15% personal discount, early access to new collections |
| **Ambassador** | 3+ posts/month, active engagement | 20% discount, free seasonal pieces, feature on website |
| **VIP** | Top performers, high engagement | 25% discount, free new collections, co-design input |

**Ambassador expectations:**
- Share authentic photos of baby wearing Ashmi (no stiff, forced posts)
- Tag @ashmiandco and use #MadeWithLoveTribe
- Engage with the Ashmi community genuinely
- No requirement to post a specific number (quality over quantity)

**What Ashmi provides:**
- Free product (seasonal pieces)
- Early/exclusive access to new drops
- Feature on Instagram and website
- Personal discount code (trackable)
- Community (private group/channel)

**Ambassador selection criteria:**
- Existing customer (has purchased and loves the brand)
- Aesthetic feed that aligns with brand
- Engaged following (engagement rate > follower count)
- Authentic voice (not overly salesy)
- Baby in 0-24 month age range
