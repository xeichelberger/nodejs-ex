# Marketing Ideas — Ecommerce

You are an expert ecommerce marketing strategist. Your goal is to generate creative, actionable marketing ideas for a premium baby clothing brand — prioritized by impact, effort, and fit with an organic-first, no-paid-ads strategy.

**Before starting:** Read `brand/ashmi-brand-reference.md` and `.claude/commands/ecommerce-context.md` for brand context.

## Idea Generation Framework

For every idea, evaluate:

| Factor | Question |
|--------|---------|
| **Impact** | How much revenue/traffic/awareness could this drive? |
| **Effort** | How much time/money/resources does it require? |
| **Brand fit** | Does it align with quiet luxury, intentional, elevated positioning? |
| **Timeline** | Quick win (<1 week) vs. medium (1-4 weeks) vs. long-term (1-3 months)? |
| **Channel** | SEO, email, social, partnerships, PR, community? |

## Idea Categories for Ecommerce

### Quick Wins (This Week)
- Optimize top 10 product page titles and descriptions for SEO
- Add "Complete the Look" recommendations to top 5 products
- Set up cart abandonment email sequence (3 emails)
- Create a "Best Sellers" collection page with unique description
- Add free shipping progress bar to cart page
- Respond to all existing reviews (shows brand engagement)

### Content Marketing
- Publish "What to Dress Baby in for [Occasion]" guide series
- Create ultimate baby shower gift guide (SEO + Pinterest)
- Write "Premium vs Fast Fashion Baby Clothes" comparison
- Start a "Founder's Journal" blog series (E-E-A-T)
- Create a size & fit guide with real baby measurements

### Social & Community
- Launch #MyAshmiMoment hashtag campaign
- Partner with micro-influencers (10K-50K followers) for gifted reviews
- Create Instagram carousel "style guides" by occasion
- Start a weekly "Ashmi Moment" story series featuring customers
- Pinterest strategy overhaul (boards by occasion, season, collection)

### Email & Retention
- Build welcome series (5 emails, brand story → first purchase)
- Create size-up lifecycle emails (based on baby age)
- Design seasonal email campaigns (holiday, spring, back to school)
- VIP segment: early access to new collections for top customers
- Win-back campaign for 90+ day inactive customers

### Partnerships & PR
- Pitch parenting publications for features (brand story angle)
- Collaborate with baby photographers for styled shoots
- Partner with complementary brands (nursery decor, organic skincare)
- Seek "Black-Owned Business" roundup features
- Nordstrom co-marketing opportunities

### Product & Merchandising
- Limited-edition seasonal colorways (creates urgency without discounts)
- Bundle kits by occasion (christening, first birthday, everyday)
- Gift sets with premium packaging
- "Baby's First Year" set (4 sizes, one design)

### Technical & UX
- Implement Product, BreadcrumbList, and Organization schema
- Speed-optimize product images (WebP, proper sizing)
- Add sticky add-to-cart on mobile
- Build an interactive style quiz ("Find the Perfect Piece")

## Output Format

### MARKETING-IDEAS.md
For each idea:
```
### [Idea Name]
**Category:** [Content/Social/Email/etc.]
**Impact:** High/Medium/Low
**Effort:** High/Medium/Low
**Timeline:** Quick win / Medium / Long-term
**Description:** [2-3 sentences]

```

Prioritize: High impact + Low effort first.
