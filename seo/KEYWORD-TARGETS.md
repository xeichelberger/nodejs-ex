## How to Read This Document

Each keyword cluster includes:
- **Est. Monthly Search Volume (MSV):** US Google searches per month (estimated ranges based on industry benchmarks and SERP analysis)
- **Keyword Difficulty (KD):** Low (under 30), Medium (30-60), High (60+) -- indicates how hard it is to rank on page 1
- **Priority:** P1 (immediate focus, weeks 1-4), P2 (weeks 5-12), P3 (months 4-6), P4 (months 7-12)
- **Intent:** Informational (I), Commercial Investigation (CI), Transactional (T), Navigational (N)
- **Current Ashmi Position:** Where shopashmi.co currently ranks (NR = not ranking in top 100)

## KEYWORD RESEARCH TOOLS RECOMMENDED

For ongoing keyword research and tracking, Ashmi & Co. should invest in:

1. **Google Search Console** (Free) -- track actual search queries driving impressions and clicks to shopashmi.co
2. **Google Keyword Planner** (Free with Google Ads account) -- validate search volumes and discover new keywords
3. **Ubersuggest** (Free tier available) -- keyword ideas, difficulty scores, and competitor analysis
4. **Ahrefs or SEMrush** ($99-199/month) -- comprehensive keyword tracking, competitor gap analysis, backlink monitoring

**Note on Search Volume Estimates:** The MSV figures in this document are estimated ranges based on industry benchmarking, SERP analysis, and available keyword research data. Exact volumes should be validated using Google Keyword Planner or a paid SEO tool before finalizing targeting decisions. The relative prioritization (which keywords to target first) remains valid regardless of exact volume numbers.
