# Unified Action Plan — Ashmi & Co. Revenue Growth

## The Goal
Aggressively scale DTC revenue toward $100K/quarter through organic channels. Zero ad spend. SEO + GEO + Community + Content as compounding growth engines.

---

## Strategic Thesis

Ashmi & Co. occupies a **unique intersection** no competitor holds: quiet luxury + Black-Owned + occasion-ready + Nordstrom-carried. The brand has the positioning. What's missing is **discoverability**.

Three compounding engines solve this:

1. **SEO + GEO** → Makes the brand findable (Google, AI search, Pinterest)
2. **Content** → Gives search engines reasons to rank and cite us
3. **Community** → Creates the third-party signals that make #1 and #2 work

These aren't separate strategies. They're one flywheel.
