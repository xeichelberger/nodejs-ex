## BLOG POST TEMPLATE

Every blog post should follow this structure for maximum SEO impact:

### Title
- Include primary keyword naturally
- Keep under 60 characters for full SERP display
- Use numbers, "guide," "best," or "how to" when appropriate

### Meta Description
- 150-160 characters
- Include primary keyword
- Include a call-to-action or value proposition
- Make it compelling enough to click

### Structure
```
H1: [Post Title -- include primary keyword]

Opening paragraph (100-150 words)
- Hook the reader with a relatable scenario
- State what the post covers
- Naturally include primary keyword in first 100 words

H2: [Section with secondary keyword]
  H3: [Subsection if needed]

[Continue with 3-6 H2 sections]

H2: Shop the Look (or similar product-linking section)
- Feature 3-5 Ashmi & Co. products with links
- Include product images

H2: Final Thoughts / Key Takeaways
- Summarize main points
- CTA: shop collection, sign up for newsletter, or read related post

Internal Links (minimum per post):
- 2 links to collection pages
- 2-3 links to specific product pages
- 1 link to another blog post
- 1 link to a page (/pages/about, /pages/gift-guide, etc.)
```

### Image Requirements
- Hero image (1200x630px minimum for social sharing)
- 3-5 in-line images featuring Ashmi products
- All images have descriptive alt text with keywords
- Image file names use keywords: "dusty-rose-baby-romper-wedding.jpg"

## COLLECTION PAGE COPY TEMPLATE

Every collection page should have optimized body copy:

```
H1: [Collection Name -- primary keyword]

Intro paragraph (100-200 words):
- Describe the collection and who it's for
- Include primary keyword in first sentence
- Mention 2-3 specific product highlights
- Include occasion/use case context

Secondary paragraph (100-150 words):
- Materials and quality details
- Size range
- What makes this collection special
- Link to related collection or gift guide

"Need Help Choosing?" CTA:
- Link to relevant blog post or size guide
- Link to related collections
```

### Example: /collections/rompers
```
H1: Baby Rompers

Discover Ashmi & Co.'s collection of premium baby rompers, designed for both
everyday comfort and special occasions. From our best-selling Rumi Romper to the
delicate flutter sleeves of our Lizzie Flutter Romper, each piece is crafted with
premium fabrics and thoughtful details that make dressing your little one a joy.

Our baby rompers come in Ashmi's signature warm palette -- dusty rose, sage green,
cream, and lavender -- perfect for family photos, celebrations, or simply
elevating everyday moments. Available in sizes 0 to 24 months, with generous
sizing that grows with your baby.

Every romper is designed at our studio in Roswell, Georgia, and ships within 1-3
business days. Looking for a coordinated outfit? Browse our [Sets](/collections/sets)
collection or add one of our signature [turban headbands](/collections/accessories).

Need help finding the perfect romper? Read our guide:
[How to Choose the Right Baby Romper for Every Occasion](/blogs/journal/baby-romper-guide)
```

## CONTENT DISTRIBUTION PLAN

Every piece of content should be distributed across channels to maximize reach:

### Blog Post Distribution
1. **Publish** on shopashmi.co/blogs/journal
2. **Share** on Instagram (carousel or Reel summarizing the post)
3. **Pin** on Pinterest (create 2-3 pins per post with keyword-rich descriptions)
4. **Email** (include in weekly/biweekly newsletter with excerpt and link)
5. **Repurpose** key stats or quotes as Instagram Stories

### Pinterest-Specific Strategy
Pinterest is a critical channel for baby clothing SEO because:
- Pins are indexed by Google and can rank in image search
- Baby clothing is one of the top Pinterest categories
- Pins drive direct traffic to product and blog pages

**Pin every:**
- Blog post (2-3 pins with different images/titles)
- Product (1 pin each with keyword-rich description)
- Collection (1 overview pin)
- Style guide (3-5 pins showing individual outfits)

**Pin titles should include target keywords:**
- "Dusty Rose Baby Romper | Flutter Sleeve | Ashmi & Co."
- "What to Dress Baby in for a Wedding | Complete Guide"
- "Premium Baby Gift Set | Luxury Baby Shower Gift"
