### Tier 3: Content-Driven Links (Highest Volume, Ongoing)

**Create Linkable Assets:**

1. **"The Complete Guide to Dressing Baby for Every Occasion"**
   - 3,000+ word definitive guide
   - Original photography
   - Downloadable checklist/printable
   - Other sites will link to this as a reference

2. **"Black-Owned Baby Brands: The Complete List"**
   - Feature other Black-owned baby brands alongside Ashmi
   - They'll share and link back
   - Becomes the definitive resource

3. **"Baby Clothing Size Guide Across Brands"**
   - Compare sizing across Kyte Baby, Quincy Mae, Carter's, etc.
   - Genuinely useful = people link to it
   - Ranks for "baby clothing size chart" queries

4. **"What Premium Baby Clothing Actually Costs (And Why)"**
   - Transparent breakdown of fabric costs, production, quality differences
   - Data-driven content gets backlinks from journalists

5. **Seasonal Gift Guides**
   - "Mother's Day Gift Guide: For the Mom Who Just Had a Baby"
   - "Holiday Baby Gift Guide: What to Give at Every Price Point"
   - Seasonal content gets picked up by press annually

### Tier 4: Relationship-Based Links (Slow Build, Compounding)

**Boutique Partner Links**
- Every wholesale/boutique partner should link to shopashmi.co
- Provide partners with brand assets and suggested link text
- Goal: 50+ boutique backlinks within 12 months (follow Quincy Mae's model)

**Influencer Blog Posts**
- Gift product to micro-influencers who have blogs (not just Instagram)
- Blog posts with dofollow links > Instagram posts for SEO
- Target: mommy bloggers with DA 20+

**Guest Posts**
- Founder story for entrepreneurship sites (Inc, Forbes contributor, Shopify blog)
- Baby fashion expertise for parenting publications
- "How I Built a Baby Brand to Nordstrom" story angle

**Cross-Promotion with Non-Competing Baby Brands**
- Nursery decor brands, baby skincare brands, baby book brands
- Joint content, joint gift guides, mutual linking
- Everyone benefits, no competitive overlap

## What NOT to Do

- **No PBN (private blog network) links** — Google and AI systems can detect these; penalty risk
- **No paid link schemes** — Violates Google guidelines
- **No mass directory submissions** — Only submit to relevant, quality directories
- **No link exchanges** — "I'll link to you if you link to me" is a red flag
- **No irrelevant guest posts** — Only write for publications your customer actually reads
- **No comment spam** — Destroys brand reputation for zero SEO value
