#### 1. Holiday Season Harvest (September-December)
- All seasonal content should be published by September for holiday searches

## APPENDIX: CONTENT PRODUCTION WORKFLOW

### For Each Blog Post

1. **Research (30 min):** Review target keyword, analyze top 5 ranking pages, identify content gaps
2. **Outline (30 min):** Create H2/H3 structure, identify Ashmi products to feature, plan internal links
3. **Draft (2-3 hrs):** Write full post following blog post template from CONTENT-CALENDAR.md
4. **Edit (30 min):** Review for keyword usage, readability, brand voice consistency
5. **Optimize (30 min):** Add meta title/description, alt text, internal links, schema markup
6. **Publish (15 min):** Upload to Shopify, submit to GSC for indexing
7. **Distribute (30 min):** Create Pinterest pins, share on Instagram, include in next email

### For Each Collection Page

1. **Create collection in Shopify** (15 min)
2. **Add products to collection** (15 min)
3. **Write optimized copy** using template from SITE-STRUCTURE.md (30 min)
4. **Set meta title and description** (10 min)
5. **Add collection to navigation** (10 min)
6. **Create internal links** from related blog posts and collection pages (15 min)
7. **Submit to GSC for indexing** (5 min)

### For Each Product Page Optimization

1. **Rewrite title tag** with format: "[Product Name] | [Product Type] | Ashmi & Co." (5 min)
2. **Rewrite meta description** with features, size range, price (5 min)
3. **Rewrite product description** using template from SEO-STRATEGY.md (15 min)
4. **Optimize image alt text** for all product images (10 min)
5. **Add internal links** to collection and blog content (5 min)
6. **Verify schema markup** is rendering correctly (5 min)
